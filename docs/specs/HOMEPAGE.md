# Homepage Content Specification

Status: First content draft  
Route: `/`  
Primary language: English

## Page job

The homepage must help the right visitor understand three things quickly:

1. Designally understands important business change.
2. Designally combines strategy and creative execution.
3. There is relevant work and a clear way to start a conversation.

The page should feel calm, premium, and visually strong. It should not feel like
a long service catalogue.

## Search preview draft

**Title:** Designally — Branding & Design Agency in Bangkok

**Description:** Designally is a strategy-led branding and design agency in
Bangkok, helping businesses create, grow, and transform through strategy,
identity, and digital experiences.

The final title and description should be reviewed after the service pages and
search targets are confirmed.

- Work

## Section 1 — Hero

### Purpose

Lead with the brand promise, then immediately explain the client problem and
what Designally does.

### Draft content

**Category label**  
Strategy-led branding and design agency · Bangkok, Thailand

**H1**  
More Than Creative.  
We Build What Matters.

**Introduction**  
Good businesses are not always seen for what they truly are. We help businesses
create, grow, and transform through brand strategy, identity, digital
experiences, and creative execution.

**Primary action**  
Tell us what's changing

**Secondary action**  
View selected work

### Visual direction

Use one strong, art-directed visual idea. It may combine selected work, motion,
or a simple brand statement. Avoid a generic agency showreel that delays the
message or creates a very large download.

### Build notes

- Render the H1 and introduction as HTML text, not text inside an image or
  video.
- The primary action links to `/contact/`.
- The secondary action links to `/works/` or the selected-work section.
- Motion must not block reading and must respect reduced-motion settings.

## Section 6 — Trust and proof

### Purpose

Reduce risk with specific facts rather than broad claims.

### H2

Built with care. Proven through the work.

### Candidate proof points

- Based in Bangkok, working across industries and markets
- 150+ brands
- Six years of work
- Thai and English collaboration
- Selected client logos

“150+ brands” and “six years” come from the 2026 brand strategy. They must be
confirmed or updated before publication. Every client logo requires permission.

### Better evidence when available

- A short approved client quotation
- A verified project outcome
- The person responsible for a project
- A real working-process detail
- A link to a complete case study

Do not use awards, partner logos, or certification marks unless they are current
and verifiable.

## Homepage answer coverage

The homepage should naturally provide short answers to these questions:

- What kind of agency is Designally?
- Where is Designally based?
- Does Designally work on new brands and rebrands?
- Does Designally design and build websites?
- Can Designally support a brand after launch?
- Can international clients work with Designally?
- How can a client start a conversation?

These answers should appear in normal page copy. Do not add a large FAQ section
only for search engines.

## Required content before design completion

- Approved hero visual direction
- Six selected and approved projects
- One-line project change statement for each selected project
- Confirmed proof points
- Approved client logos and quotation, if used
- Final office and contact information
- Three reviewed launch articles or a decision to hide Insights until ready
- English homepage copy approval
- Thai homepage content plan

## Acceptance checks

- The value proposition is understandable within the hero and next section.
- The page uses one H1 and a logical heading order.
- All important information works without animation, hover, or audio.
- Every link has a real destination.
- Mobile content order matches the story priority.
- Project media is selected and optimised.
- The page has valid metadata, canonical URL, and social sharing image.
- Organisation or local-business structured data matches visible company facts.
- Keyboard, focus, contrast, reduced motion, responsive layout, and performance
  have been tested.
