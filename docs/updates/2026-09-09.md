# 9 September 2026 — Situations section, paper plane, hover cursor

## The homepage's "next section" placeholder is gone

It held the literal text "next section" and existed only to carry the colour
handover from the hero. It now holds the **situations section**: the heading
"Where are you now?", the statement beside it, and three cards.

The section keeps `bg-primary-300`, as the placeholder's own comment required.
Every colour inside it is an on-accent one — the block came from a white section
and its dark inks would have been unreadable on brand orange.

The white `#situations` section it came from was removed, and the `id` moved
with the content so any existing anchor still resolves. Nothing linked to it.

### The cards

Three 4:5 cards, each arriving from below, turning face-up, and then filling in
with its copy. The artwork is real: `public/situations/cardframe-back.png` and
three fronts, 800x1000 each, copied from the design library.

**Which face carries which artwork is not arbitrary, and it took three attempts
to get right.** The card settles at `rotateY(180)`, so the face carrying
`rotate-y-180` has come round a full 360 by then and faces the viewer squarely.
The artwork belongs THERE. Put on the plain face — which is the one turned away
at rest — it is only ever seen from behind, and renders mirrored. Both the
illustrations and the "D" card back were shipped mirrored at different points
before this was understood.

Two related notes now in the file: neither face may carry `overflow-hidden`, and
the faces are ordered so paint order favours the resting one if
`backface-visibility` ever fails.

The whole card is one link. `after:absolute after:inset-0` stretches the
existing `TextLink` over the article rather than wrapping the card in a second
`<Link>` — two links to the same place mean a screen reader announcing the
destination twice per card. That overlay is also why the hover lift is
`group-hover` and not `hover`: the pointer is never over the card visual itself.

## Paper plane

A plane flying a supplied 6798x2393 path as the statement section scrolls past,
scrubbed directly to scroll position — no autoplay, no loop, reverses on the way
back up.

**The path and the plane share one SVG coordinate system.** Two SVGs would have
meant translating the plane's position between two spaces on every frame and
every resize, which is where this kind of thing drifts. In one `viewBox`,
MotionPathPlugin measures both in the same units and the whole thing is
responsive for free.

Two things were got wrong first and are worth recording. The trigger was the
plane's own layer, which deliberately overhangs its section — so the flight
started a screen early; it is timed to the host section now, and `FLIGHT_REACH`
(where it flies) is independent of `SCROLL_START`/`SCROLL_END` (when). And the
layer had to be widened past both screen edges, or the path's ends landed ON
them and the plane appeared already parked rather than flying in.

## Hover cursor

The pointer is replaced by a circle with an arrow over the showreel, the work
showcase and each situation card.

Three defects in the first version, all instructive:

- `overwrite: true` on the fade tween killed the position tweens mid-flight,
  which read as the circle stalling and jumping. It is `"auto"` now.
- `gsap.set` behind `quickTo`'s back left its internal start stale. Repositioning
  passes the destination as the start instead.
- The rectangle was only re-read on `pointermove`, so a Lenis scroll with a
  still pointer left the circle behind. A frame loop reads it now.

`offsetParent` is read once per hover rather than per frame: it forces a layout,
and on the showcase that landed on the same frames the drift loop writes a
transform.

## Hero gradient

The hero's cream-to-orange gradient now dissolves into the solid orange beneath
it as the sticky stage scrolls, finishing exactly as the pin releases — which is
the moment the statement section's bottom reaches the fold. Derived from the pin
rather than from a fraction of the stage, so it follows the layout if a section
is added or resized.

## Section layout

Case Study, situations and "how we think" now share one grid: title on columns
1-4, content on 7-12, `gap-6 lg:gap-8`. `SectionTitle` changed from a
`justify-between` flex row to that grid, because the flex row sized both halves
from their contents and the gutter moved with the heading's length.

Placement is stated as start and end LINES everywhere. `col-span-*` compiles to
`grid-column: span N / span N`, a shorthand that rewrites both ends and silently
overrode a `col-end-*` written beside it.

"How we think" moved from brand orange to cream, which meant more than a `bg-`
class: `data-header-tone="light"` came off (it asks the floating logo to turn
white), both white borders became `border-border-default`, and its `TextLink`
lost `tone="inverse"`.

## Component API changes

- `MaskedText` gained `as` — a heading has to stay a heading, and a `<p>` inside
  an `<h2>` is invalid HTML that browsers "fix" by closing the heading early.
- `TextLink` gained `size` (`sm` / `md` / `lg`) and `sweep`. Size is a prop
  because `text-sm` was in the base classes and a call site overriding it would
  be settled by stylesheet order.
- `DoubleChevronIcon` gained `tone` (`brand` / `current`), so the mark can sit
  on a coloured surface where its own oranges would vanish.
- `BrushUnderline` added, from supplied artwork, with `currentColor` in place of
  the `#F56341` the source carried.
