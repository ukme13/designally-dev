# 8 September 2026 — Work showcase, section title, adaptive logo tone

## Homepage work showcase

A full-bleed row of the four approved posters in the section below the sticky
stage, drifting leftward.

**Height is the fixed dimension, not width.** Each card is sized by `HEIGHT` and
takes its width from the image's own ratio, so a 16:9 poster and a 4:5 portrait
can share the row without either being cropped or letterboxed. That is
groundwork: these images come from a CMS later and nothing there guarantees a
shared aspect ratio.

Three copies of the set, not two. The offset wraps after one sequence, so the
track has to stay wider than the viewport at that moment. Two covered a viewport
narrower than one sequence — about 2144px at `xl` — and a wider display ran out
of track and showed a gap before the jump.

The trailing padding on each sequence is arithmetic, not decoration: the gap
between the last card of one copy and the first of the next has to equal the gap
inside a copy, or the seam stutters once a pass. `GAP` and its `pe-*` half move
together.

### Drag, and why the pointer handlers write nothing

The row can be dragged by mouse or finger. The handlers do not touch the
transform — they bank movement for the next frame, so the frame loop stays the
only writer and drag, drift and scroll push cannot fight over one style.

While a pointer is down the row is entirely under the hand: drift and scroll
push are zeroed rather than ignored, so releasing resumes from rest instead of
firing off a speed banked before the grab. Release hands the hand's own speed to
a fling that decays back into the drift.

`touch-pan-y` is what makes this work on a phone — the browser keeps vertical
panning for the page and leaves horizontal movement to us. `setPointerCapture`
keeps events coming when a fast drag outruns the cursor, and `draggable={false}`
stops the browser's native image drag stealing every event after the first.

An earlier attempt set `data-lenis-prevent` statically. That told Lenis to
ignore the wheel over the row *always*, so the page would not scroll whenever
the pointer crossed it. It is now set only in the reduced-motion branch, where
the container really is a horizontal scroller.

## Homepage section title

`section-title.tsx` — the old site's "Case Study" heading, ported from
`designally-clone/src/components/sites/designally-co-e422ade5/shared/CaseStudySection.tsx`
rather than rebuilt from a screenshot. The first attempt was built from an image
and got the mark wrong: it is a two-tone `))` chevron, not the circular monogram.
Both of its hard-coded hexes turned out to be palette entries already —
`#F78267` is `primary-250` and `#F56341` is `primary-300` — so the drawing came
across unchanged and now takes its colour from tokens.

Two things the port had to change:

**The heading is composed from three utilities, not one.** Every `type-*`
utility at heading size resolves to EB Garamond through `--font-display`; the
Poppins ones stop at `type-h2`, 18→20px. So the size token is taken on its own
and the family and weight stated beside it — `font-sans text-accent-xl
font-medium` — three utilities owning three properties, with nothing overriding
anything.

**The mark is sized in `em`.** The original is a 40px mark beside 40px type with
24px between them; `h-[1em]` and `gap-[0.6em]` hold that proportion wherever the
clamp lands, instead of needing breakpoints.

One bug worth recording: a bare text node inside a flex container becomes its own
anonymous flex item, so `Case <span>Study</span>` was two items and the row's
`gap` landed between the words as well as after the mark. The title is now
wrapped in a single span.

The note beside the title became a `<div>` rather than a `<p>`, because it holds
a `ScrollGradientText` — which renders its own `<p>` — and a nested paragraph is
invalid HTML that browsers "fix" by closing the outer one early.

`ScrollGradientText`'s sweep now ends on `"center center"` rather than
`"bottom 35%"`, so the last character lands as the paragraph sits centred
regardless of its height or the scroll speed.

`TextLink` gained an opt-in `sweep` prop carrying the header's underline wipe,
plus `focus-visible` — the header has hover only, and a link whose sole
affordance is hover is invisible to anyone tabbing to it.

## Adaptive header logo tone

The floating header's monogram turns white over dark and brand-orange sections.
Sections declare themselves with `data-header-tone="light"`; nothing samples
pixels. Full reasoning in `ADR-004`.

Five markers on the homepage: the sticky stage, the solid `primary-300` section,
the top third of the fading showcase band, `#work`, and the orange section
before Services. The band gets a partial marker because it fades to
`surface-base` by its bottom, where a white logo would be worse than the problem.

## Refactor

`showcase-loop.tsx` went from 370 lines to 155 by moving its motion into
`app/_lib/use-showcase-drift.ts`, the same split `hero-intro.tsx` has with
`use-statement-flight.ts`. The component is markup and layout knobs; the hook is
speed, coupling and throw.

The rendered DOM was captured before the refactor and compared after: **byte for
byte identical**, 97,333 characters both times. The only differences in the built
page are chunk filenames and module ids in the RSC payload, which change because
the module graph did.
